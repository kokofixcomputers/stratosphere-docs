---
title: Schedules
description: Build cron schedules with live explanations, next run times, validation and presets in the schedule window.
---

The schedule window builds a cron schedule for a server without you having to remember what each field means. As you type, the panel explains what the schedule does.

## Live explanations

The five cron fields are explained as you fill them in, in plain sentences such as:

- "Runs every 5 minutes."
- "Runs at 04:00 on weekdays."

Under that, the window shows the **next three times the schedule runs**, by the clock of the panel. The timezone is the panel's `APP_TIMEZONE` (handed to the page as `timezone`), so the times match how the panel counts.

## Validation

A wrong value is explained the same way, for example "Minute must be between 0 and 59." While a field is wrong, the **save button waits** until it is fixed.

## Presets

**Presets** fill the fields with a click, so common schedules (every few minutes, once a day, on weekdays, and so on) do not have to be typed from scratch.

## Cron syntax

Only plain cron is read:

- numbers,
- lists,
- ranges,
- steps,
- `JAN`-`DEC` month names,
- `SUN`-`SAT` day names,
- `0` and `7` both mean Sunday.

`L`, `W` and `#` are passed through to the panel's own check on save, but the window does not explain them. When **both** the day of the month and the day of the week are set, either one is enough, like cron does.

## Windows that fit

The windows and cards no longer run off the bottom of the screen. The space above a window is a share of the **height** (never the width), and a window that is taller than the screen **scrolls inside itself**.
