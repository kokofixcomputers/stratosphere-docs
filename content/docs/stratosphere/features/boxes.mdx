---
title: Boxes
description: Resources you own that you make your own servers in, and the store that sells them.
---

A **box** is memory, CPU, disk, ports, backups and databases that belong to you. You make servers **inside** a box and choose how much of the box each server gets. What a server does not use stays in the box for the next one.

A box is made by an administrator for you (no payment needed), or bought in the store. Servers that are not in a box keep working as before.

## Using a box

The **Boxes** icon in the top bar (it shows once you have one) lists your boxes. Open one to see what it holds, how much the servers in it have been given, and the servers themselves.

- **Create a server** picks a name, a node (when the box is not tied to one) and a share of the box with sliders. The server starts like every server you make yourself, install Minecraft from its Versions tab.
- **Resources** next to a server changes its share. Taking some away gives it back to the box, you can only give a server what the box has left.
- Deleting a server gives its share back.
- A box that ran out of time, or that an administrator suspended, has its servers stopped. Renew it (see below) or ask the administrator.

The ports of a server are the ports it may have, the first one is made when the server is, the rest you add on its Network tab.

## Buying and renewing

The **Store** icon appears when the administrator opened the store. Put what you want in the **cart** (Add to cart on the package, the cart icon in the top bar shows how many things are in it). The cart page lets you change how many of each, take things out and see the total, then you pay for all of it at once with Stripe or PayPal. The cart stays in your browser until you pay, the prices are the ones of the store when you pay.

A package is a **box** or a **server**: a server is made for you when you pay, and you do what you like with it. You pay on the page of Stripe or PayPal, the panel never sees your card. When you come back the panel asks the payment service whether it was paid and makes what you bought.

A payment lasts for the days of the package. A box or server that was bought has a **Renew** button on its page: the time is added to what is left. What is not renewed is **suspended**, never deleted, and renewing brings it back.

For administrators, see [Boxes and the store](/docs/stratosphere/admin/boxes).
