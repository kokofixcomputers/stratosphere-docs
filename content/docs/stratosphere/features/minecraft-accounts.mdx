---
title: Minecraft accounts
description: Link a Minecraft account to your account, use its skin as your avatar and be invited to servers by name.
---

A person can link their Minecraft account to their Stratosphere account. The skin head becomes their avatar, and other people can invite them to a server by Minecraft name instead of an email address.

## Linking

1. Join the address the administrator gives on the **Account** page (Minecraft Java Edition, a real account, not offline mode).
2. You are disconnected with a screen that has a 6 digit code. It works for 15 minutes.
3. Enter the code under **Account > Minecraft account**.

The account page then shows the Minecraft name and the head of the skin. **Unlink** removes it again. A Minecraft account can be linked to one account of the panel only. If the Minecraft account is renamed, the new name is picked up the next time it joins the link address.

## Inviting by name

When the administrator turned linking on, **New User** on the Users page of a server has two choices: **Email address** and **Minecraft name**. Type a name and the head shows up as soon as the name is found. Only people who linked their account can be invited by name, and the Users list shows their Minecraft name and head.

## How it is checked

The Minecraft router (`stratosphere-net`) answers an address that leads to no server with the same login steps an online mode server uses. The player's game proves with Mojang that the account is theirs, and only then does the router ask the panel for a code. An offline mode or cracked client cannot get one. Nothing is stored on the router.

For administrators: turn it on under **Admin > Settings > Minecraft accounts**, see [Minecraft accounts](/docs/stratosphere/admin/minecraft-accounts).
