## Root admins and roles

The admin area is split between **root admins** and ordinary users who have been given **roles**.

- **Root admins** are the accounts that were admins before roles existed. They can do everything and cannot be limited.
- Everybody else is an ordinary user until a role is given to them. A role is a name, a color and a set of permissions grouped by area.

The sidebar only shows the pages a person can open, and they land on the first page they may open. Changes to a role apply to its members on their next request. See [Roles](/docs/stratosphere/admin/roles).

## Where the Stratosphere pages live

Most Stratosphere-specific admin work is under **Admin > Settings**, which holds the CurseForge, Authentication, Providers, Domains, Certificates and Trash tabs, and on the **Roles**, **Extensions** and **Updates** pages of the sidebar.

<Callout type="info">
  The admin area is built with **AdminLTE** and has no dark theme yet, unlike the rest of the panel. See [Theme](/docs/stratosphere/features/theme).
</Callout>
